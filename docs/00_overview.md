# App Specification Overview

## Product working name

Home Meal Planner

## Core problem

Households that cook at home frequently struggle with the recurring question:

> What should we eat today?

This creates decision fatigue, coordination issues, grocery gaps, and missed advance-prep tasks such as soaking, fermenting, marinating, chopping, or thawing.

## Product vision

Build a shared household meal-planning assistant that recommends practical meals based on household preferences, diet, family size, cooking time, variety rules, groceries, prep requirements, and member permissions.

## MVP promise

The MVP should help a household:

1. Set up a household food profile.
2. Save and resume lengthy preference setup.
3. Invite household members or temporary guests.
4. Generate today's meal suggestion.
5. Generate a weekly meal schedule.
6. Replace or reject meals.
7. Mark a meal as eating out.
8. Generate grocery lists.
9. Show advance-prep reminders.
10. Notify household members when the menu or schedule changes.

## MVP personas

### User

A household member who wants to plan, view, change, or receive updates about meals.

### Household owner

The user who creates the household and manages members, permissions, and household-level preferences.

### Guest

A temporary household participant, such as a relative or friend staying for a few days.

### Administrator / Operator

Internal persona who manages the dish database, ingredients, tags, prep metadata, and dish quality.

## Non-goals for MVP

The MVP should not initially focus on:

- Full pantry inventory automation
- Receipt scanning
- Grocery delivery integrations
- Advanced medical nutrition recommendations
- WhatsApp bot automation
- AI-only freeform recipe generation
- Complex family voting workflows
- Full calorie/macro tracking
- Multi-country localization
