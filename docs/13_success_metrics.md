# Success Metrics Specification

## Objective

Measure whether the app actually reduces meal decision fatigue and improves household meal planning.

## Activation metrics

### Onboarding completion rate

Percentage of users who complete household setup.

Goal:

- MVP: 40%+
- Later: 60%+

### Draft resume rate

Percentage of users who resume incomplete onboarding.

Goal:

- MVP: 20%+

### First meal generated

Percentage of users who generate at least one meal suggestion.

Goal:

- MVP: 70% of completed profiles

## Engagement metrics

### Weekly active households

Number of households that open the app weekly.

### Meal plan usage

Percentage of households with at least 3 planned meals per week.

### Meal acceptance rate

Percentage of recommended meals accepted.

### Meal replacement rate

Percentage of suggested meals replaced.

This is not always bad. It shows interaction.

### Eating-out marking rate

Tracks whether users use realistic meal status management.

## Recommendation quality metrics

### Accepted suggestion rate

Higher means recommendations are useful.

### Rejection reason distribution

Track reasons:

- Not in mood
- Too much effort
- Ingredients unavailable
- Repeated recently
- Kids will not eat
- Too unhealthy
- Too expensive

### Repeat complaint rate

How often users reject because of repetition.

### Prep miss rate

How often a meal cannot be cooked because prep was missed.

Goal:
Reduce over time.

## Household collaboration metrics

### Invite sent rate

Percentage of households that invite at least one member.

### Invite acceptance rate

Percentage of invites accepted.

### Shared household activity

Number of household actions by non-owner members.

### Permission denial rate

How often users try actions they cannot perform.

## Grocery metrics

### Grocery list generation rate

Percentage of weekly plans that generate grocery lists.

### Grocery item checked rate

Percentage of items checked off.

## Retention metrics

### Day 1 retention

User returns next day.

### Day 7 retention

User returns after one week.

### Household retention

Household uses app for at least two consecutive weeks.

## North star metric

Recommended:

> Number of household meals successfully planned and acted on per week.

A meal is considered successfully acted on if it is:

- accepted
- cooked
- marked eating out
- intentionally skipped
- replaced by user

## Qualitative questions

Ask beta users:

1. Did this reduce "what should we eat today?" discussions?
2. Were the suggestions practical?
3. Did prep reminders help?
4. Was the grocery list useful?
5. Did shared household view help coordination?
6. What made you reject a suggested meal?
7. What meal did the app suggest that felt wrong?
